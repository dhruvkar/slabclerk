Everyone opens a different email.

Every seat gets its own briefing, built from that person's own book and sorted by the dollars at stake. There's nothing to install and nothing to learn. Your people just get an email, and nothing goes out without an OK.

To: You

The whole yard: every leak, plus whether yesterday's flags actually got worked.

To: Each rep

Their book, sorted by money: quiet accounts, unanswered proformas, holds about to expire. The follow-ups come drafted in their name.

To: The office

Who to call today: the invoices worth the most, certs about to expire, and which accounts to pause before their next pickup.

To: Purchasing

What's on the water and what's running thin: track & trace on every container, arrival notices matched to their POs, reorder drafts, and the quarry orders that need chasing.

To: The warehouse

Which trucks go where: tomorrow's delivery plan built from open pick tickets, loading worksheets in load order, and a flag on anything sold that still has no truck date.

That's maybe a third of the list. Markdowns, win-backs, discount drift, lot mismatches, receiving claims... ask for the full sheet.

Hit reply.

The morning email is a line to the clerk behind it. Anything you'd normally do in your system one screen at a time, you can just ask for in a sentence.

Price 3cm Taj Mahal for Elite Granite — their tier, delivered.
What did we actually clear on the last Alaska White container, landed?
Raise Fantasy Brown 2cm list 6% and send the reps the new sheet.
Offer the 280-day bundles at 20% off to every fabricator who's bought quartzite this year.
Collection notices to everyone past 60 days — skip the two on payment plans.

Each one comes back done or drafted: bundles found, buyers matched, forty emails sitting in the queue for your OK.

It takes attachments too. Forward a proforma and packing list and the PO gets entered line by line. Forward the supplier's invoice and it gets matched against that PO, with anything off flagged. Freight bills get booked into landed cost. The typing is the clerk's job now.
